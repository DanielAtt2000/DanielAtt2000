Wheel Alignment in Malta

Computerised wheel alignment in Siġġiewi for cars, 4x4s, vans and multi-axle trucks.

If your vehicle pulls to one side, your tyres wear unevenly, or you've just had steering or suspension work done, call us to arrange an alignment check.

Call 7965 5577 to book

Vehicles we align

  • Cars and other light vehicles
  • 4x4 and four-wheel-drive vehicles
  • Vans
  • Multi-axle trucks, cranes and coaches

What we check

We measure the wheel angles using computerised equipment and check them against the vehicle's alignment specifications. Correct alignment helps improve handling and reduce uneven tyre wear.

For commercial vehicles and fleets, tell us the vehicle type when you call so we can arrange the right appointment.
